# Chess for iPad

Play chess in Safari on your iPad — versus the computer or a friend on the same device.

## Play on iPad

1. Open the game in **Safari**.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. After the first load it works offline.

Source: this repository. If GitHub Pages is enabled, the game is at:
https://spartanxjoe.github.io/chess-ipad/

Until Pages is enabled you can open the files locally or use:
https://raw.githack.com/spartanxjoe/chess-ipad/main/index.html

## How to play

- Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square.
- Gear button: vs computer or two-player, Easy / Medium / Hard.
- Toolbar: New, Undo, Flip, Hint.

Full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate.
